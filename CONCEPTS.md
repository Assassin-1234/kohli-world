# KOHLI●WORLD: the dot is the world

Design notes: why the site looks and moves the way it does.

## The idea

The dot in the domain becomes the world. The site opens on the wordmark **KOHLI●WORLD** with a halftone planet sitting where the full stop would be, and the whole site is one continuous camera move around that planet.

Every part of the planet does a real job, and each job is one part of a life:

| Chapter | Body | What it does | What it stands for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrival | the dot | sits in the wordmark | a small world under construction |
| Premise | the whole planet | turns behind blurred type | *Built. Defended. Expanding.* |
| I. Surface | the crust | where you live | 10m air rifle: the impact site (10.9, a perfect shot) is where the precision came from |
| II. Shield | a geodesic layer | gets attacked first, by us | PMG-CSR: red attack streams hit the shield and weak points are flagged with red squares |
| III. Core | a cutaway | generates the field that keeps the shield up | the gym: iron core, trained daily |
| IV. Satellite | the moon | orbits, waits, pulls the tides | the trading system: the orbit ring doubles as a price trace |
| V. Star | the sun | lights everything, gets seen | SunnySideUp: a halftone sun rising behind the giant type |
| VI. Night side | city lights | stay on after the sun sets | the people: three equal lights, one per person; hover a name to brighten their light |
| Landing | the end card | | Population 01, circled in red marker |

The parts depend on each other the way real physics does: the core generates the field that makes the shield possible. That is why it reads as one world rather than a list of sections.

## Moves borrowed from print and film

- **One object carries the whole site.** Scrolling moves the camera, not the page.
- **Screen-space halftone with colour fringing**, so the 3D looks printed rather than rendered.
- **Huge condensed type eaten away by noise** instead of fading. Outgoing and incoming type never overlap.
- **Type behind the object, out of focus**, like a camera's depth of field.
- **Show the process, don't describe it:** attacks hit the shield, the core is cut open, the orbit is a chart.
- **A pixel-mosaic loader** that resolves into the first frame.
- **Tiny labels with corner ticks** and a live lat/lon crosshair, like an instrument.
- **One human mark:** a hand-drawn red circle at the very end.

The direction was inspired by the Neuroform site: one object, halftone rendering, eroding type.

## Language

- **Type:** one family, Archivo, narrowest cut for display, wider cuts for labels and body. Four sizes only: label, body, name, display.
- **Colour:** cream `#F3E8D2`, ink `#0A0605`, signal red `#E0241B`, and a hot orange for light. The background shifts per chapter: blood red, black, deep red, black, sunrise orange, night.
- **Rendering:** everything 3D is halftone, with radial colour fringing and grain in one post pass. No glows, no glass, no gradients for their own sake.
- **Motion:** the planet always turns forward, an even step per chapter, on a spring. It drifts a little when idle and leans with scroll speed; colour fringing grows with rotation speed.
- **Copy:** first person, plain, no slogans, no em dashes.

## Easter eggs

Two, on purpose: one anyone can find, one for people who read source and headers. Neither is required, and neither is explained on the site.
