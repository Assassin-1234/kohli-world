I. Surface

The
surface

I started shooting 10m air rifle at 15 and went on to compete at international level, with a national bronze along the way.

At 19 I stopped. I wanted to do one thing properly, and I chose building. I’d like to shoot again someday, when I have the time.

If shooting taught me one thing, it’s that consistency is boring, and it’s also the only thing that ever turned my potential into results. I still build everything that way.

II. Shield / PMG‑CSR

We attack
first.

I started Audexis to build software where the outcome matters beyond the screen. We’ve built classified AI/ML systems for defence and government.

PMG‑CSR comes next: an autonomous penetration-testing platform for defence supply chains and critical national infrastructure. Within an authorised scope, it probes systems the way a real attacker would, so the weak points get found and fixed first.

It’s the work I believe in most, and the one I plan to spend years on.

V. Star / SunnySideUp

Manya and I are building SunnySideUp: AI-powered influencer marketing, starting with cars. Every campaign starts with research into the market, the brand and the product. The data picks the concept and the creators, then we run it end to end.

The interesting part isn’t generating AI influencers. It’s rethinking how a whole campaign gets built, with research, creative direction, creator discovery and execution working as one process.
